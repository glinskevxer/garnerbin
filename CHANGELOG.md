# Changes

## Unreleased

- Premium scales run through the settlement, so a house that pays for protein
  and discounts for damage does both to the same price.
- The sample file and the scale tests are on the exception sheet.

- The bid sheet, so the number on the wall and the number the settlement uses
  are worked out in one place.
- Grain bank accounts, with the shrink taken on the way in.
- Journal replay, so an audit can work forwards from the sealed entries.
- Season summaries with the shrink and discount margin broken out.
- Sample retention with a schedule, so a disputed sample is still in the drawer.
- Scale tests in divisions, with the drift priced at a working load.
- Truck loading against the gross, axle and bridge limits at once.
- Freight and netback, ranking destinations by what they pay at the pit.
- Minimum price contracts, with the floor worked out as cash less premium.
- Advances against unpriced grain, with interest on actual days.
- Grower accounts and the payables book.
- Wheat protein on the twelve percent basis, falling number and vomitoxin.
- Aeration decided on equilibrium moisture, and temperature cable readings read
  for shape rather than for absolute degrees.

## Earlier

The first working version covered receiving, grading against the federal tables,
shrink and dockage, settlement against a posted schedule, contracts of all six
kinds, storage, the bin ledger, the daily position, the hedge book and rail
loading, with a command line over the lot.
